Current preference: A for compactness.

A depends on confirming the compact cell's supplier drawing and performance. B needs supplier-trimmed battery tabs and welded leads. Lowering the battery does not guarantee better stability when the heavier motors move upward. Larger wheels also reduce wheel force at a given torque.

Both passed nominal CAD checks. Battery protection, full wiring, contact retention, structural details and tolerances remain to be engineered.
